# 11 business models

Prices from each company's official pricing page, read 2026-10-31 (source: Build to Own Master, week 2). Prices change often; open the link for the current one.

| # | Model | Group | How it charges | Example | Public price | Fits |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Seat subscription | Pay for time used | Per user per month | [Notion](https://www.notion.com/pricing) Plus, Business | $10 to $20 per user per month | Tools a whole team uses daily |
| 2 | Freemium | Pay for time used | Free to attract users, pay to lift limits | [Spotify](https://www.spotify.com/us/premium/) Premium Individual | $12.99 per month | Very large markets |
| 3 | Open core | Pay for time used | Open source, charge for hosted and enterprise | [Supabase](https://supabase.com/pricing) Pro | from $25 per month | Dev tools that need a community first |
| 4 | Usage-based | Pay for usage | Per call, token or transaction | [Stripe](https://stripe.com/pricing), US domestic cards | 2.9% + 30¢ per transaction | Usage that varies by customer |
| 5 | Outcome-based | Pay for usage | Charge only on a result | [Intercom Fin](https://www.intercom.com/pricing) | $0.99 per outcome | Repeated work with measurable results |
| 6 | One-time license | Sell once, sell rights | Pay once, use forever | [ShipFast](https://shipfa.st/) Starter, All-in | $199 to $249 once | Builders who need a code base |
| 7 | White label | Sell once, sell rights | Rebrand and resell | [GoHighLevel](https://www.gohighlevel.com/pricing), SaaS Mode on the $497 plan | $97, $297, $497 per month | Agencies that want their own software |
| 8 | Productized service | Sell finished work | Fixed monthly price, clear scope | [Designjoy](https://www.designjoy.co/) Monthly Club | $4,995 per month | Solo operator, steady customer demand |
| 9 | Service-as-Software | Sell finished work | Software does the work, you deliver the result | [ListKit](https://listkit.io/) | software $597 per month; done-for-you from $18,000 per quarter (third-party source) | Customers who want the result and hate doing it |
| 10 | Marketplace | Charge on connection | % of revenue that flows through | [Affitor](https://www.affitor.com/) | 3.5% of affiliate revenue after the first $10,000 | Platforms joining two sides |
| 11 | Community, cohort | Charge on connection | Membership or program fee | [Skool](https://www.skool.com/pricing) | Hobby $9 + 10% transaction fee; Pro $99 + 2.9% | People who already have an audience |

- One product can combine models, e.g. seat-based software plus a monthly done-for-you plan.
- Pick the model by how the customer wants to pay, not by what is easiest for you to bill.
